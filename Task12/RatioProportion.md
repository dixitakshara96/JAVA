## Solutions and short tricks

### Q1. Combine two ratios

Given (A:B=2:3) and (B:C=4:5).

Make the common term (B) equal. The LCM of 3 and 4 is 12:

-   (A:B=8:12) (multiply by 4)
-   (B:C=12:15) (multiply by 3)

**Answer: (a) 8 : 12 : 15**

**Shortcut:** Match the middle term before combining ratios.

### Q2. Fourth proportional

If (a:b=c:d), then (d=`\frac{bc}{a}`{=tex}).

\[ 4:9=12:x `\Rightarrow `{=tex}x=`\frac{9\times12}{4}`{=tex}=27 \]

**Answer: (a) 27**

### Q3. Mean proportional

Mean proportional between (a) and (b) is (`\sqrt{ab}`{=tex}).

\[ `\sqrt{12\times75}`{=tex}=`\sqrt{900}`{=tex}=30 \]

**Answer: (a) 30**

### Q4. Third proportional

\[ x=`\frac{36^2}{16}`{=tex}=`\frac{1296}{16}`{=tex}=81 \]

**Answer: (a) 81**

### Q5. Divide money in a fractional ratio

\[ `\frac12`{=tex}:`\frac13`{=tex}:`\frac14`{=tex} \]

### Q7. Make four numbers proportional

For proportional numbers, (`\frac{a}{b}`{=tex}=`\frac{c}{d}`{=tex}).

Let the number added be (x):

\[ `\frac{6+x}{15+x}`{=tex}=`\frac{20+x}{43+x}`{=tex} \]

\[ (6+x)(43+x)=(15+x)(20+x) \]

Cancel (x\^2) on both sides and simplify:

\[ 258+49x=300+35x `\Rightarrow`{=tex}14x=42 `\Rightarrow `{=tex}x=3 \]

**Answer: (a) 3**

### Q10. Milk and water --- step-by-step

A 45-litre mixture has milk : water (=7:2).

**Step 1: Find the total parts.**

\[ 7+2=9`\text{ parts}`{=tex} \]

**Step 2: Find milk and water.**

Each part (=45/9=5) litres.

-   Milk (=7`\times5`{=tex}=35) litres
-   Water (=2`\times5`{=tex}=10) litres

**Step 3: Add only water.**

Milk stays at 35 litres. Let (x) litres of water be added. The new ratio
must be (5:2):

\[ `\frac{35}{10+x}`{=tex}=`\frac52`{=tex} \]

Cross-multiply:

\[ 70=50+5x `\Rightarrow20`{=tex}=5x `\Rightarrow `{=tex}x=4 \]

**Answer: (a) 4 litres**

**Important trick:** When water alone is added, the quantity of milk
does not change. Keep milk fixed and change only the water quantity.

**Quick check:** New mixture = 35 L milk + 14 L water = 49 L. Ratio
(35:14=5:2), correct.

### Q14. Partnership profit
