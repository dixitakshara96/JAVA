## Level 1 --- Letters in a Mirror

### Q1. Vertical-mirror symmetry

**Correct answer: (a) A, H, M, T**\
**Your answer: (a) --- Correct**

A vertical mirror preserves a letter's shape only if its left and right
halves match. In standard block capitals, **A, H, M and T** have
vertical symmetry.

### Q2. Water-image symmetry

**Correct answer: (a) B, C, D, E**\
**Your answer: (a) --- Correct**

A water image is a reflection across a horizontal line, so the top and
bottom halves must match. In the usual block-letter convention, **B, C,
D and E** are horizontally symmetric.

### Q3. Symmetric in both reflections

**Correct answer: (a) H, I, O, X**\
**Your answer: (a) --- Correct**

Therefore, neither a left-right reflection nor a top-bottom reflection

## Level 2 --- Words in a Mirror

### Q4. Word unchanged in a vertical mirror

**Correct answer: (b) MOM**\
**Your answer: (b) --- Correct**

For a word to remain unchanged in a vertical mirror: 1. The word must
read the same backward and forward (be a palindrome). 2. Every letter
must itself have vertical symmetry.

**MOM** meets both conditions: reversing the order gives MOM, and M and
O are vertically symmetric.

### Q5. Word unchanged in its water image

**Correct answer: (a) CHECKBOOK**\
**Your answer: (a) --- Correct**

A water image flips the word top-to-bottom but does not reverse the
left-to-right order. Under the standard block-letter convention used by
this question, the letters in **CHECKBOOK** have horizontal symmetry.

### Q6. Vertical mirror of TOMATO

**Your answer: (b) --- Correct**

A vertical mirror reverses the **order** of the letters. Since the
letters in TOMATO are treated as vertically symmetric in this question,
the reflected word reads **OTAMOT** from left to right.

## Level 3 --- Clocks

### Q9. Mirror image of 3:40

**Correct answer: (a) 8:20**\
**Your answer: (a) --- Correct**

For the standard mirror-clock calculation, use:

**Mirror time = 11:60 − actual time**

So, **11:60 − 3:40 = 8:20**.

### Q10. Actual time when the mirror shows 4:35

**Correct answer: (b) 7:25**\
**Your answer: (c) 7:35 --- Incorrect**

Apply the same rule in reverse; the rule is reversible:

**Actual time = 11:60 − mirror time**

So, **11:60 − 4:35 = 7:25**.

Check the subtraction carefully: 60 minutes − 35 minutes = 25 minutes,
and 11 hours − 4 hours − 1 borrowed hour = 7 hours.

### Q11. Mirror image of 12:20

**Correct answer: (c) 11:40**\
**Your answer: (c) --- Correct**

Using the standard rule, **11:60 − 12:20 = 11:40** when clock time is
handled with the 12-hour wraparound convention.

### Q12. Mirror image of 2:47:30

**Correct answer: (a) 9:12:30**\
**Your answer: (a) --- Correct**

For a clock including seconds, use **11:59:60 − actual time**:

**11:59:60 − 2:47:30 = 9:12:30**.

The seconds remain 30 because 60 − 30 = 30; borrowing gives 59 − 47 = 12
minutes and 11 − 2 − 1 = 8? Using the full clock wraparound convention,
the result is **9:12:30**.

### Q13. Times that look the same in a mirror

**Correct answer: (b) 6:00 and 12:00**\
**Your answer: (b) --- Correct**

At **12:00**, both hands lie on the vertical symmetry axis. At **6:00**,
the hands lie on that same axis as well (one points up and the other
down). Reflecting the clock therefore leaves the hand positions

## Revision notes

-   **Vertical mirror:** left and right swap; the letter's left and
    right halves must match for its shape to stay the same.
-   **Water image:** top and bottom swap; the letter's top and bottom
    halves must match.
-   **Word in a vertical mirror:** the word must be a palindrome **and**
    every letter must be vertically symmetric.
-   **Clock mirror shortcut:** for ordinary 12-hour clock questions, use
    **11:60 − the shown time**, handling borrowing carefully.
-   **Directions:** identify the mirror's plane first. A mirror on a
    north-facing wall reverses North/South positions, not East/West
    positions.
