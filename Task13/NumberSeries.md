## Level 1 — Difference & Multiply

### Q1. 7, 12, 17, 22, 27, ?
**Correct answer: (b) 32**

Each term increases by 5:
7 + 5 = 12, 12 + 5 = 17, 17 + 5 = 22, 22 + 5 = 27, so 27 + 5 = **32**.

### Q2. 2, 6, 18, 54, ?
**Correct answer: (c) 162**

Multiply each term by 3:
2 × 3 = 6, 6 × 3 = 18, 18 × 3 = 54, so 54 × 3 = **162**.

### Q3. 4, 6, 10, 16, 24, ?
**Correct answer: (a) 34**

The differences are +2, +4, +6, +8. The differences increase by 2, so the next difference is +10.
24 + 10 = **34**.

### Q4. 1, 3, 7, 15, 31, ?
**Correct answer: (a) 63**

Each term is multiplied by 2 and then 1 is added:
1 × 2 + 1 = 3
3 × 2 + 1 = 7
7 × 2 + 1 = 15
15 × 2 + 1 = 31
31 × 2 + 1 = **63**.

## Level 2 — Squares, Cubes & Primes

### Q5. 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?
**Correct answer: (d) 37**

These terms follow n² + 1:
1² + 1 = 2, 2² + 1 = 5, 3² + 1 = 10, 4² + 1 = 17, 5² + 1 = 26.
The next term is 6² + 1 = **37**.

### Q6. 0, 7, 26, 63, 124, ?
**Correct answer: (a) 215**

These are cubes minus 1:
1³ − 1 = 0, 2³ − 1 = 7, 3³ − 1 = 26, 4³ − 1 = 63, 5³ − 1 = 124.
The next term is 6³ − 1 = **215**.

### Q7. 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29, ?
**Correct answer: (c) 31**

The sequence lists consecutive prime numbers starting from 11. The next prime after 29 is **31**.

### Q8. 3, 4, 8, 17, 33, ?
**Correct answer: (b) 58**

The differences are +1, +4, +9, +16 — consecutive squares: 1², 2², 3², 4². The next difference is 5² = 25.
33 + 25 = **58**.

## Level 3 — Mixed & Alternating

### Q9. 1, 2, 6, 24, 120, ?
**Correct answer: (a) 720**

Multiply by consecutive integers:
1 × 2 = 2, 2 × 3 = 6, 6 × 4 = 24, 24 × 5 = 120.
Next: 120 × 6 = **720**.

### Q10. 2, 5, 14, 41, 122, ?
**Correct answer: (d) 365**

Multiply by 3 and subtract 1 each time:
2 × 3 − 1 = 5
5 × 3 − 1 = 14
14 × 3 − 1 = 41
41 × 3 − 1 = 122
122 × 3 − 1 = **365**.

### Q11. 2, 10, 4, 20, 8, 40, 16, ?
**Correct answer: (b) 80**

There are two alternating sequences:
- Odd positions: 2, 4, 8, 16 — each term doubles.
- Even positions: 10, 20, 40, ? — each term doubles.
So the missing term is 40 × 2 = **80**.

### Q12. 1, 3, 4, 8, 15, 27, ?

1 + 3 + 4 = 8
3 + 4 + 8 = 15
4 + 8 + 15 = 27
The next term is 8 + 15 + 27 = **50**.
